Before you hand over the phone

  • Turn the ringer and notifications off — a banner mid-trial invalidates it.
  • Screen brightness to a fixed level, and the same level for every participant.
  • On iPhone, launch this from the home-screen icon, not from Safari.
  • The first trial is practice and is flagged as such in the data.
