Stand in the office

A real room, rebuilt from a phone video. You stand in the middle of it and can't walk — turn your head (or the phone) to look around.

  • In the viewer, press its button to re-centre the view on whatever is in front of you.
  • Without a viewer, move the phone around — or drag, on a laptop.
  • On iPhone, launch this from the home-screen icon, not from Safari.
